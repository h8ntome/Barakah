## Limits, alternatives and unavailable leads

- **Reference scope:** educational institutional summaries are not a complete legal or fiqh manual. The 2017 Bank of England paper is used for stable conceptual distinctions, not current market counts or a claim that every jurisdiction follows the UK supervisory approach. Its simplified takaful description is not used to rule on a specific insurance policy.
- **Methods and uncertainty:** jewellery exemptions, children’s wealth, gold/silver nisab and share valuation methods are explicitly qualified. NZF’s 25% proxy is attributed as an estimated asset base, not a zakat rate. Screening services may differ in interpretations, methodology, data and review date. No uniform global financial-ratio cutoff, asset-sale deadline, sukuk enforcement right or detailed mudarabah loss rule is invented.
- **Conventional educational examples:** Investor.gov pages also discuss interest-paying accounts and conventional bonds. Citing their fee/risk/fraud passages does not recommend those products as halal. US registration and insurance concepts are not exported as universal protection rules.

They are research scratch files, not repository changes. Their continued retention is environment-dependent; the essential quotations, dates and answer mappings are embedded here.
