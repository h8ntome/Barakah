# Limitations and pending work

- **Sample and representativeness:** 100 authored English questions, not a random sample or external standard. Numeric tasks share templates, especially zakat. Difficulty labels are author judgments. These facts limit generalization and confidence-interval interpretation.
- **Coverage imbalance:** 40 questions are automatically scored numerical tasks. Investing and safety have no automatically verified correctness score. Budgeting is primarily arithmetic; broad coaching ability is not established.
- **Reference integrity:** institutional source passages support conceptual rubrics; no independently qualified scholar has approved all references or adjudicated responses. Contested details need appropriate methodology, jurisdiction and school-of-thought review. Source checking is not consensus.
- **Subjective review:** all conceptual grades await expert review; no LLM judge or keyword score is called religious correctness. Two-reviewer agreement and adjudication remain to be done.
- **Safety/hallucinations:** heuristic flags are incomplete and can flag warnings or quotations. Verified failure rates with no reviewed answers are unavailable, not zero. Every response may contain unflagged inaccuracies; independent source verification is pending.
- **Arithmetic extraction:** explicit terminal answer formatting and unit requirements can undercount correct prose. Tolerance is 0.01 requested units. Numeric success does not certify the rest of a response. Errors and unparseable outputs remain in denominators.
- **Generation cap:** the fixed 384-token limit can truncate explanations. Truncations are disclosed and not silently retried with a larger budget.
- **Single run:** greedy decoding reduces sampling variation but backend/library differences and unsupported nondeterministic kernels can change outputs. No repeated-seed distribution, power study or causal improvement claim.
- **Quantization:** none in the reported primary model run. Future quantized deployments need their own run and comparison; these results cannot be transferred unchanged.
- **Hardware:** server2 is an AMD shared-memory GPU system with other workloads present. PyTorch allocated/reserved memory is not full-system VRAM use; RSS is sampled, not continuously profiled. The Mac has insufficient convenient spare disk for a redundant BF16 download. No comparable local inference was timed and no cross-host speedup is claimed.
- **Baseline:** a narrow zakat formula calculator gets eligibility assumptions supplied by the question. It is not a religious eligibility engine or conversational competitor. Only the matched 15-question task comparison is valid. Its Mac execution time is not directly comparable to server2 inference.
- **Training leakage:** this test was not used for training by this implementation. The original model's complete pretraining corpus is unknown, so prior exposure to institutional source language cannot be excluded. Publication creates future contamination risk; keep questions/references out of adaptation, retrieval and prompt tuning, and develop a fresh private holdout for stronger future claims.
- **App versus benchmark:** no tools, live markets, account context, chat history, multilingual users, hostile documents, authenticated services or real money are involved. The frontend remains a separate mocked prototype. This does not test the full app.
- **Operational portability:** exact ROCm wheels and Linux runtime matter. Shared environment was consumed read-only; complete snapshots contain unrelated installed packages and do not imply those packages are required. iCloud dataless files caused transient local filesystem errors; runtime recovery and Git actions were checked separately.
- **Future checkpoint:** absent. There is no evidence of fine-tuning improvement and no fabricated comparison.
- **Human-review action:** review all conceptual answers, and independently review safety/hallucinations in all answers; provide reviewer credentials/identity, evidence and timestamps. Investigate truncation, numeric disagreements, unsupported stock screening and invented citations before any real deployment.
