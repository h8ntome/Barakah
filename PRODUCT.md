# Barakah

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React 18, Vite, lucide-react, standard CSS, client-side state. Specified by the user.

## Users
Young adults and households learning faith-aligned personal finance, including beginners unfamiliar with zakat, nisab, and investment screening.

## Product Purpose
Islamic Finance for a Blessed Tomorrow. Help people understand their sample finances, estimate zakat, explore Sharia-conscious investing, plan sadaqah, and ask a local demonstration finance guide.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five destinations: Home, Invest, Zakat, AI Chat, Give. All service operations must use local asynchronous mock data. No backend, credentials, real payments, live markets, inference, authentication, database or infrastructure. The backend belongs to a separate teammate. USD is a prototype choice; religious rules and integration schemas are provisional. A dedicated frontend branch must be committed and pushed to the configured team repository if access permits.

## Brand Commitments
Barakah spelling; emerald, mint and off-white palette; refined serif wordmark; mosque arch/crescent logo; approachable, respectful, financially credible voice. The user explicitly pins the visual world and composition: an emerald desktop editorial panel and one interactive phone, becoming edge-to-edge on mobile.

## Evidence on Hand
`barakah-ui/reference/barakah-concept.png` supplied concept image; existing React UI in `barakah-ui/src/`. No verified securities, charities, religious rulings, or personal financial data.

## Product Principles
- Make all demo actions real frontend interactions, never pretend they moved money.
- Teach without implying a fatwa, certification or personalized advice.
- Isolate mock data and replaceable services from page layouts.
- Keep financial totals coherent when assets change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
320px to desktop; readable text, visible focus, labeled fields, keyboard navigation and dialogs, reduced-motion support, loading/error/retry/empty states. Avoid a single universal scholarly method or regional threshold.

## Open Decisions
Backend team must approve contracts, currency/FX policy, religious methodology, privacy, charity vetting and any future financial integrations. No new product assumptions are needed for this frontend delivery.
