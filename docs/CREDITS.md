- **Barakah checkpoint:** [h8notme/barakah1](https://huggingface.co/h8notme/barakah1), full checkpoint revision `912a5697cf9d85adf065ee512f509cfa236459d4`, not a separate adapter. Its card names LFM3 and credits [Unsloth](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth) and [TRL](https://github.com/huggingface/trl). Do not treat the card as permission to remove upstream conditions or redistribute commercially. No weights are included in the repository or submission package.
- **Verified training dataset (also used for the historical overlap screen):** [Azfarhashmi/adaption-personal-finance-advice-dialogues](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Azfarhashmi/adaption-personal-finance-advice-dialogues), revision `3dd7f33149d6cbf4ab9115f76242851506929b9d`, 27,981 public rows. Credit Azfarhashmi as publisher and **Adaption's Adaptive Data platform** for remastering, as the [pinned card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Azfarhashmi/adaption-personal-finance-advice-dialogues/raw/3dd7f33149d6cbf4ab9115f76242851506929b9d/README.md) states. The card specifies `license: []`, not a license grant. Saved runs select the whole Hub train split without slicing and no evaluation split; its row labels include test/val, so this does not establish held-out independence. Training stopped at 4413/6996 steps (1.26 epochs), not two completed epochs or proven unique exposure to all rows. The limited lexical overlap screen is not proof of independence; no corpus is redistributed in the package. [Current provenance and preserved historical evidence](../personal_finance/CURRENT_PROVENANCE.md).
- **Original evaluation:** 100 AI-assisted authored synthetic English scenarios; 40 numeric references independently recalculated in code; 60 conceptual answers unreviewed. [Dataset card](../benchmark/datasets/DATASET_CARD.md), [reference evidence](../benchmark/datasets/SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md), [audit](../benchmark/rescue/evidence/original_numeric_audit.json).
- **Exploratory personal-finance evaluation:** 50 AI-assisted authored synthetic scenarios, 23 strict numerical-field checks. Created after the original aggregate comparison; frozen before its own inference. All human advice-quality scores remain null. Rubric changes and canonical packet adoption are disclosed in [the report](../personal_finance/REPORT.md). It is not an independent confirmatory test.
- **Live demo:** synthetic user questions and isolated fictional app data only; not anyone's real finances. Recorded assistant text is genuinely generated, including adverse answers. Prompt policy changes are development work, not evidence of an evaluated safety gain.
