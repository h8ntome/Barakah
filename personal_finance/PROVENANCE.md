# Creation and lineage provenance

- Release: Personal Finance Domain Adaptation Benchmark v1.0, authored in English on 2026-11-15 UTC by the implementation AI assistant at the user's direction. This is not independently human-authored or human-validated. No benchmark model outputs were read during question, reference, rubric or rule creation; the existing original benchmark's model answers were not accessed.
- The 50 scenarios were composed for this release, with 10 budgeting and 8 each saving, debt, decisions, literacy and conversations. Conversation cases contain a previous statement and a changed constraint in one user message, not interactive multi-turn execution. Topics are personal-finance domain adaptation, not an Islamic-finance superiority test.
- Numeric references use Python Decimal, not either evaluated model or inference tools. Source-named tests independently express the 23 calculations from scenario facts. The other 27 cases have no automatic numeric score. Human references and rubric anchors are authored guidance, not completed independent expert review.
- Candidate training corpus: `Azfarhashmi/adaption-personal-finance-advice-dialogues`, public HF revision `3dd7f33149d6cbf4ab9115f76242851506929b9d`. Public metadata and file inventory were retrieved without credentials. The existing local corpus `/tmp/homefinance-corpus/train_0.jsonl` was read only, never copied. Its 354098432 bytes and SHA256 `95f46af8ee1b4981aaa85a94fcb0701e51c27b987c2e6be1936bfc21fd103446` match the public immutable-revision LFS inventory. All 27,981 rows were screened across string fields, including prompts, completions and reasoning traces.
- This corpus was identified by the user/supervisor as a plausible candidate from prior project context. **It is not confirmed to be the actual barakah1 training set.** The pinned candidate model card confirms LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B as the base and mentions Unsloth/TRL, but names no training corpus, row selection, split or training hyperparameters. A scoped read-only server2 probe of `/home/hadi/homefinance-benchmark-lfm3` configuration/docs and shallow training/notebook paths did not locate an actual fine-tuning row manifest. No claim of an exhaustive server search is made.
- `evidence/overlap.json` records full-corpus number-masked trigram containment screening, with a 0.55 flag threshold and exact number-masked scenario substring checks. Zero exact matches and zero threshold flags were found; maximum containment 0.24444444444444444. The author inspected the top ten ranked excerpts before freezing and saw generic topic overlap, not obvious scenario reuse. This is not an independent semantic review and cannot exclude nonlexical paraphrases, hidden training data or pretraining contamination. Training excerpts were accessed only after initial scenario composition; no scenario was copied from them.
- The immutable full checkpoint manifests are the preexisting copied files under `../benchmark/results/hardware/`. Models are loaded from server2's cached snapshots and every manifest file is rehashed before use. No downloads of model weights, retraining, weight modifications, original-repository writes, commits or pushes are permitted.
- Freeze manifest covers questions, rubric, numeric rules, inference config, provenance, scripts, tests, evidence and the copied checkpoint manifests/dependency scripts. Results and post-run report are deliberately excluded. The manifest itself is SHA256-hashed in `freeze_manifest.sha256`; inference records its hash. Hashes provide integrity/provenance, not an external trusted timestamp.
- Human subjective scores must remain null until real blinded review occurs. Packets omit model identities and are randomized; mapping is stored separately. Unmodified generated text could self-identify, and small-model style may also weaken blinding.
